Help an engineering calculator read handwriting

I'm an independent developer building a free engineering calculator: you write an expression by hand, units included (like 2 kN/m or 20 °C), and it works out the answer. It struggles with handwriting that isn't mine, because everything it has learned from is a few people's writing. I'm collecting ordinary handwriting from lots of different people. It takes about ten minutes. No account, no name, no email.

The lines you copy look like L := 6 m or M/S = MPa. Some are deliberately nonsense, so you don't need to understand them: just copy the shapes.

What happens to it

  • Your handwriting is used to train and test handwriting-recognition models.
  • The project is free and personal now but may become a commercial product. There is no payment.
  • Your samples are not published or resold, and I don't collect your name or email.
  • You must be 18 or over. You can ask for your samples to be deleted by emailing inkmath-project@proton.me.

You will see and agree to the full agreement before you start, on whichever route you pick. That agreement, not this summary, is what applies.

Thank you. Copying these 15 lines by hand takes about ten minutes, and it teaches a maths-reading program what real handwriting looks like — something it currently only knows from one person.

What you are agreeing to

  • You must be 18 or over to take part.
  • You are giving your handwriting to this project to train and test handwriting recognition models.
  • The project is currently free and personal, but may become a commercial product. Your permission covers that, with no further approval needed and no payment to you.
  • Your photographs are not published or resold. They are used to train and test models, and may be described in aggregate.
  • Location data is removed. Phone cameras record where a picture was taken, usually your home, plus your phone's make and model. We then strip that data before anything is stored or used, normally within a few days.
  • Do not write anything personal. Copy only the printed expressions. Do not include your name, and keep faces, documents and anything identifying out of the frame.
  • Taking part is voluntary. To have your photos deleted, email inkmath-project@proton.me with your pack number and the reference code you chose, if any. Photos already used to train a model cannot be taken back out of that model, but they will not be used again.

How to do it — about 10 minutes

  1. Print this page, or keep it open on a screen beside you, and write on any blank unlined paper.
  2. Copy each expression exactly as printed, and write its item number in the left margin. The number is how your handwriting gets matched to what it says, so it must be legible.
  3. Leave a clear gap between items — at least a finger's width. Expressions that touch each other are nearly useless to us. This matters more than neatness.
  4. Write naturally, at your normal size and speed. Please do not tidy your handwriting for the camera. Ordinary handwriting is the whole point; neat handwriting is not.
  5. Made a mistake? Cross it out and carry on. Real pages have crossings-out and we want them.
  6. Photograph the page with your phone: page filling most of the frame, as square-on as you can manage, and good light. One photo per page. Hold the phone the same way for every photo.
  7. Upload the photos using the link at the bottom of this page, and enter your pack number when asked. If you like, choose a reference word of your own there, so you can ask us to delete your photos later.
